Support

Something confusing, broken or delightful? I'd love to hear it: drawnapp.feedback@gmail.com

Testing with TestFlight

Take a screenshot in Drawn and share it from the TestFlight prompt, or open the TestFlight app, choose Drawn, and tap Send Beta Feedback. Or just email me.

Deleting your account

In Drawn, go to Friends → Add a friend → Delete my account. This deletes your account and your data from our server.

Keep your projects

In this test version your projects live on your phone. Deleting the app erases them, so update rather than delete and reinstall.
