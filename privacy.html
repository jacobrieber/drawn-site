Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-15

Drawn is made by one person, Jacob Rieber. This page explains what the app stores and why.

What's stored on your phone

Your projects, work sessions, notes and photos. They stay on your device.

What's stored on our server

So that friends can see your progress, the app keeps a copy of the following on our server, hosted by Supabase:

  • your project names (a project marked private shows to friends only as "Private Project")
  • session start and end times
  • finished-project notes and photos
  • the display name you choose
  • your time zone
  • your invite code
  • your friend connections
  • lanterns sent and received

Your account is anonymous: there's no email or password, and we don't ask for your real name.

Who can see it

Only you, plus the friends you accept. Friends see your display name, your projects (private ones without names, notes or photos), your hours, your finished lanterns, and whether you're working right now. Someone you haven't accepted yet sees only your display name and hours.

Screen Time

If you use app blocking, the apps you choose stay on your phone as Apple's private tokens. We never see which apps they are.

No ads, no tracking, no selling

Drawn has no advertising or analytics tools and never sells or shares your data.

Deleting your data

In Drawn, go to Friends → Add a friend → Delete my account. This deletes your account and your data from our server. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: drawnapp.feedback@gmail.com
